# Evidence semantics

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not support |
|---|---|---|
| AST pattern | A source-level risk worth checking | Observed nondeterminism or causal diagnosis |
| Repeated comparable outcomes | An intermittently-failing observation | Independence of attempts or known root cause |
| Rule risk index | Consistent triage across transports | Failure probability |
| CatBoost raw score | A prediction under its versioned feature schema | Calibrated probability without a separate calibration study |
| LLM explanation | An advisory hypothesis tied to supplied evidence IDs | Verified fix or independent label |
| RAG cosine distance | Distance under the pinned embedding/index setup | Percentage match or diagnostic confidence |
